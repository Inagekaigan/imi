04 / RESEARCH NOTES

Evidence before
confidence.

What runs. What was measured. What remains uncertain.

The model

Loading local model information…

Sentence classification, ten semantic categories, error-token localization and experimental scope supervision. The encoder reads both sentences together.

The three outcomes

Meaning altered: the calibrated estimate crosses the positive decision threshold.

Meaning preserved: the estimate crosses the negative threshold. A model judgment, not certification.

Uncertain: neither threshold is met, or the pair exceeds the supported length. Abstention is never counted as a successful classification.

Training & limitations

Google and TexTra translations with human MQM annotations from MTPEdocs-MQM, plus authored contrastive examples. Document-separated training, validation, calibration and test sets. Natural documents are municipal public information; authored examples have not been independently reviewed.

Only the binary sentence score is temperature-calibrated. Category and token scores are uncalibrated. Highlights are predicted error tokens, not verified causal explanations or word alignments. Pairs over 192 model tokens abstain without truncation.

Research use: upstream annotations are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and exclude rights to source documents. Code, data and model licensing are separate. Read the repository’s data-license notes before redistribution or commercial use.

Measured results

Three seeds compare ordinary fine-tuning and auxiliary scope supervision. The research report includes category scores, confidence intervals and negative results. This is a pilot, not a state-of-the-art claim.

Your text stays here

Inference runs in a local CPU process. No API keys, uploads or automatic feedback collection. Optional feedback saves the pair and prediction separately on this device; it never automatically enters training or evaluation. History and feedback are stored unencrypted under your Windows profile.
